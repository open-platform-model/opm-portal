## Why

opm-portal installs OpenSpec 1.12.0 locally and in CI, while the other repos move to 1.14.1. The generated OpenSpec skills in `.claude/skills/` are at `generatedBy: "1.12.0"`. The bump aligns the portal with the rest of the workspace.

`--strict` leaves `task openspec:check` for now. Version 1.14.1 reports a requirement longer than 500 characters as a WARNING. `--strict` fails on any warning, and no flag turns this one check off. The portal has 85 such requirements in its main specs. A later change rewrites them and puts `--strict` back.

## What Changes

- **Pin.** `Taskfile.yml` (`OPENSPEC_VERSION`) and `.github/workflows/lint.yml` install `@fission-ai/openspec@1.14.1`, in lockstep.
- **Gate.** `task openspec:check` runs `openspec validate --all --no-interactive`. Its `desc`, the line in `AGENTS.md`, the line in `openspec/config.yaml` and the comment in `lint.yml` say that `--strict` is off until the rewrite lands. The `lint.yml` step is named "OpenSpec"; it is a step of the required `Lint` job, not a check of its own.
- **Skills.** The eleven generated `openspec-*` skills are regenerated by 1.14.1 (`generatedBy: "1.14.1"`). The local edit of `openspec-verify-change` (Task Hygiene block, naming lines) is merged in by a three-way merge.

Release class: none. CI, task and skill files only; no Go code changes.
